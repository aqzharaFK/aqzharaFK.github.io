Meet the Makers

Behind every stitch is a person who believes in doing things the right way. These are the hands, hearts, and heads behind Americana Pipedream.

Wyatt Callahan

Founder & Creative Director

Born on a dirt road in Tennessee, Wyatt built APA from a pickup truck and a dream. He designs every piece with the open road in mind.

Mae Holloway

Head of Design & Textiles

Mae sources every fabric by hand, traveling the country to find the mills that still do it the old way — slow, careful, and built to last.

Cody Briggs

Master Craftsman & Production

With 20 years on the workshop floor, Cody oversees every step of production — from cut to stitch to finish — refusing to rush quality.

Della Stone

Brand & Community Lead
